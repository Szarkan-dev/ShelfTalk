========================================================================
 ShelfTalk — ReadingCircle Book Club & Review Suite (Direct Launch)
========================================================================

HOW TO OPEN:
Just double-click 'index.html' to open ShelfTalk in your browser!

- NO Node.js required
- NO live server / localhost required
- NO command line, PowerShell, or cmd required
- 100% self-contained and portable across Windows, macOS, Linux, and ChromeOS!

FEATURES AVAILABLE OUT-OF-THE-BOX:
1. Curated 16-title literary catalog with genre filters & search
2. Author spotlights for 15 distinguished authors
3. Accessible 5-star rating engine with keyboard navigation
4. Real-time dynamic score recalculation (4.4 -> 4.5 stars)
5. Reading circle personal page tracker with animated circular SVG rings (69%)
6. Chapter discussion forums with spoiler blur shields (click to reveal)
7. Annual reading goal odometer ('14 of 25 Books Read')
8. Digital quote highlighter & margin notes scrapbook
9. 3-question book recommendation quiz
10. Exportable / printable literary journal

LocalStorage persists your reviews, progress, and quotes across browser sessions.
Click 'Reset Demo Data' in the navigation bar to reset to the original seed data anytime.
